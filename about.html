Our Story

FROM A CORNER STORE TO A POWERHOUSE

2019

The Beginning

The Health House opens its first store in the Netherlands — a small sports nutrition shop with a big vision: make premium supplements accessible with personal, expert advice.

2020

Rapid Growth

Despite the pandemic, word spreads fast. Customers love our personal approach. We expand to multiple locations across the Netherlands and launch our online store.

2022

Market Leader

The Health House becomes the Netherlands' #1 sports nutrition chain with 20+ stores, 100+ employees, and partnerships with top European brands.

2025

International Expansion

We set our sights on Southeast Asia. Thailand is chosen as our first international market — with Bangkok, Chiang Mai, and Phuket as launch cities.

2026

Thailand Launch

The Health House Thailand opens its doors. The same quality, the same personal service, the same passion — now in the heart of Southeast Asia. 🇹🇭
